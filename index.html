How we workAn operating model

Design the work.
Govern the agents.

AI changes more than who does a task. We design how people, systems, and agents collaborate; what they may access; what the organization remembers; and how each cycle can improve the next. Our Acceleration Packs apply this point of view in specific domains, not the limits of our work.

Security is part of the work.

Give each agent a purpose and owner. Limit its authority, approve where sensitive data can go, require human approval for consequential actions, and revisit controls as work changes.

↗

Choose the outcome.

Name the business KPI, establish its baseline and target, and decide who owns the result.

↗

Design the collaboration.

Handoffs and decision rights matter as much as task assignment. Define how teams, systems, and agents work together and when a person steps in.

↗

See how the work happens.

Observe handoffs and actions across people, systems, and agents. Trace decisions so failures can be investigated rather than guessed at.

↗

Keep memory actionable.

Preserve decisions, evidence, and outcomes so the next team or agent can recover why a choice was made, with access appropriate to the information.

↗

Turn evidence into improvement.

Track the KPI after implementation against its baseline and target. Automate repeatable checks where possible; use the findings to change workflows and controls.

Return

Measured results set the next priority.

The Codentient viewAn argument, in four moves

Output scales. Attention doesn't.

I

The backlog moves into production.

Software is already plentiful. AI makes more of it viable to build, including tools shaped around a company's own work. Projects once left waiting for engineering capacity can now ship.

II

Human attention is the limit.

One person can only carry so many projects. Hiring more people does not make attention limitless. As output grows, decisions, review, and accountability demand a more deliberate use of human effort.

III

The division of labor changes.

Human attention moves up — to intent, judgment, authority, consequences. Machines take on what people never could: continuous memory, correlation, verification, routine execution.

IV

Govern the impact, not the change.

Not more gates. Failure kept small, visible, short-lived: hard constraints before, limited blast radius during, fast detection after. Governance at the speed of the system it controls.
